# Download checklist: paywalled and library items

Merged from the three verified reading lists
([reflection theory](reading_list_reflection_theory.md),
[holography and dislocations](reading_list_holography_dislocations.md),
[ptychography](reading_list_ptychography.md)), where the details, DOIs and reasons are given.

* **Where to save:** put every PDF into `literature_pdfs/` in your clone. That folder is git-ignored,
  so the files are never uploaded.
* **File names:** `Author_Year_shortname.pdf` (for example `Osakabe_1989_PRL.pdf`). For a book
  chapter, use `Author_Year_book_chN.pdf`.
* **Books:** download only the chapters listed.
* **Before uploading anything:** check that your library licence allows uploading book chapters to an
  AI tool.

## Tier 1: the foundation (get these first)

### Reflection diffraction theory (what our multislice must reproduce)
- [ ] **Ichimiya & Cohen**, *Reflection High-Energy Electron Diffraction* (CUP 2004),
      DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511735097: **ch. 4, 5, 11, 12, 14 and App. F**. Library e-book.
- [ ] **Peng, Dudarev & Whelan**, *High-Energy Electron Diffraction and Microscopy* (OUP 2004):
      **ch. 5, 13 and 14**, plus the list of appendices. Library e-book.
- [ ] **Peng & Cowley**, Surf. Sci. 199, 609 (1988) and Acta Cryst. A42, 545 (1986): the method our
      kernel implements. Library journal.
- [ ] **Kirkland**, *Advanced Computing in Electron Microscopy*, 3rd ed. (2020): **ch. 4 and 6**, the
      atomic-potential appendix, and a scan of the contents pages. Library e-book.
- [ ] **Peng, Ren, Dudarev & Whelan**, Acta Cryst. A52, 257 and A52, 456 (1996): our scattering
      factors and absorption. Library journal.

### Reflection electron holography (the original experiments to compare with)
- [ ] **Osakabe et al.**, Phys. Rev. Lett. 62, 2969 (1989), **plus the erratum** in PRL 63, 584 (1989).
- [ ] **Osakabe et al.**, Ultramicroscopy 48, 483 (1993), and **Banzhof & Herrmann**,
      Ultramicroscopy 48, 475 (1993).

### Electron holography method
- [ ] **Völkl, Allard & Joy (eds.)**, *Introduction to Electron Holography* (Springer 1999):
      **ch. 6** (reconstruction, pp. 125–151), **ch. 13** (diffracted-beam holography, pp. 295–310)
      and **ch. 15**. Library e-book.
- [ ] **Hÿtch et al.**, Ultramicroscopy 111, 1328 (2011): dark-field holography of strain.
- [ ] **Wang et al.**, Microscopy Today 33(6), 43 (2025): dark-field holography of dislocations, the
      closest modern paper to our topic.

### Dislocations (the elastic field in our sample)
- [ ] **Head**, Proc. Phys. Soc. B 66, 793 (1953): a dislocation near a free surface, the exact
      theory behind our field.
- [ ] **Hull & Bacon**, *Introduction to Dislocations*, 5th ed. (2011): **ch. 4–6**. Library e-book.
- [ ] **Hirth & Lothe** (or Anderson, Hirth & Lothe, 3rd ed. 2017): **ch. 3 and 13**, plus the
      **table of contents and index pages**, so that we can find the free-surface chapter.

### Ptychography (the next engine)
- [ ] **Maiden & Rodenburg**, Ultramicroscopy 109, 1256 (2009): ePIE.
- [ ] **Maiden, Humphry & Rodenburg**, JOSA A 29, 1606 (2012): multislice ptychography.
- [ ] **Chen et al.**, Science 372, 826 (2021): the published version **with the supplementary
      materials**. A free arXiv version also exists: 2101.00465.
- [ ] **Van den Broek & Koch**, PRB 87, 184108 (2013) and PRL 109, 245502 (2012): inverting
      multislice.
- [ ] **Thibault et al.**, Science 321, 379 (2008), with the supporting material.

## Tier 2: strengthens specific points

- [ ] **Z.L. Wang**, *Reflection Electron Microscopy and Spectroscopy for Surface Analysis* (CUP
      1996): **ch. 6** (then ch. 3, 4 and 9 and App. B). Covers how steps and dislocations look in
      reflection.
- [ ] **McCoy & Maksym**, Surf. Sci. 310, 217 (1994) and 297, 113 (1993): earlier simulations of
      step contrast on Si(001).
- [ ] **Kruse et al.**, Ultramicroscopy 106, 105 (2006): the mean inner potential of Si.
- [ ] **Ma & Marks**: erratum Acta Cryst. A46, 626 (1990), and **Ma**, Acta Cryst. A47, 137 (1991).
- [ ] **Maksym & Beeby**, Surf. Sci. 110, 423 (1981); **Ichimiya** (JJAP 1983, and the 1985
      correction); **Ishizuka**, Acta Cryst. A38, 773 (1982); **Yao & Cowley**, Ultramicroscopy 33,
      237 (1990); **Peng & Cowley**, Ultramicroscopy 29, 168 (1989).
- [ ] **Ting**, *Anisotropic Elasticity* (OUP 1996): **ch. 5 and 8** (the half-space Green's
      function is on p. 260).
- [ ] **Balluffi**, *Introduction to Elasticity Theory for Crystal Defects* (CUP 2012): **ch. 13**.
- [ ] **Lehmann & Lichte**, 2002; **Tanji, Ito & Yada**, Ultramicroscopy 35 (1991).
- [ ] **Edo et al.**, PRA 87, 053850 (2013); **Takahashi et al.**, PRB 87, 121201 (2013);
      **Holm** 2025 (M&M abstract); **Ichikawa & Doi** 1988.
- [ ] **Rodenburg & Maiden**, the published Springer Handbook chapter (pp. 819–904); the free
      accepted manuscript is in the free list below.
